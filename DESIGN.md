# Design System: Share Finance

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Note Series"**

Every kutu group is printed as its own banknote series. The security features people check on a real note by habit (guilloche linework, a rosette, microprinted rules, a see-through window, a serial) are how this product shows that everyone sees the same thing. The note face is the one saturated object on a screen. Around it, the app is set like a bank's paperwork: cool paper, ink-black text, hairline rules and tabular figures.

Density is calm and ledger-like. Lists, tables and the payments grid are ruled in ink and neutral, never tinted. Hierarchy comes from a fixed size ramp in which the pot figure is always the largest thing on the page. Motion is limited to printing. The note prints in once, one rosette petal inks for each paid member, and the microprint rule prints across. After that, the surface stays still.

Night print is the dark theme. It keeps the same world with the paper and ink inverted, and it lifts each series so the note still reads as ink on paper.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cool paper ground (paper tokens), ink text, hairline rules.
- One series colour per group, used only on its note face and on small note-shaped swatches.
- Status is always a mark plus a label, never colour alone.
- Three typefaces with strict jobs: Archivo for UI, Bodoni Moda for pot figures, Martian Mono for real serials.
- One solid-ink primary action per screen.
- No KPI tiles and no pills.

## Colors

The palette is cool, near-neutral paper and ink, with six series inks kept to note faces and a small set of status inks that always appear next to a mark.

### Primary
- **Press Ink** (action / ink): the reserved colour. Used as a solid fill only on the one primary action per screen (for example "Pay your RM 500.00"), on the skip link, and on the member's own avatar. In night print it becomes pale ink on dark paper (night-action).

### Secondary: the six note series
Each series is a triplet. The **ink** colours text and petals on the note, the **tint** is the note's paper, and the **line** is the guilloche and underprint linework. Series are assigned from a hash of the group id (`src/lib/series.ts`), so a group has the same colour for every member on every device.
- **Series 0, Treasury Indigo** (series-0-*)
- **Series 1, Crimson Note** (series-1-*)
- **Series 2, Deep Teal** (series-2-*)
- **Series 3, Violet Issue** (series-3-*)
- **Series 4, Magenta Print** (series-4-*)
- **Series 5, Charcoal Issue** (series-5-*): a deliberate neutral. Every remaining saturated hue collided with paid green, late amber or another series.
- **Specimen** (specimen-*): neutral graphite. It is used when there is no group id yet (wizard preview) and only ever appears with a SPECIMEN stamp.

### Tertiary: status inks
- **Paid Green** (paid): filled seal, contribution amounts in the ledger, and the "issued" stamp.
- **Late Amber** (late): half-filled seal, overdue due-dates, the owes ring in the rail, and warning notices.
- **Failed Red** (failed, failed-tint): struck ring, danger buttons, field errors, failed notices, and the "held" stamp.

### Neutral
- **Cool Paper** (paper): page ground.
- **Raised Sheet** (paper-raised): fields, notices, modal, secondary buttons, the active rail item.
- **Sunk Paper** (paper-sunk): hover wells, avatars, disabled primary.
- **Ink 2 / Ink 3** (ink-2, ink-3): secondary text and metadata; ink-3 also marks placeholders and quiet column heads.
- **Rule / Rule Strong** (rule, rule-strong): hairline dividers and control borders. Rule-strong also inks the not-due-yet dot.
- **Focus Blue** (focus): focus outlines, caret and selection only.

### Named Rules
**The Note-Only Rule.** Series colour lives only on note faces and small note-shaped swatches (the rail's 12x16px swatch and the ledger's 8x11px group swatch). Tables, lists, chips, tabs and current-round highlights stay in ink or neutral.

**The Reserved Ink Rule.** Solid ink fill marks the single primary action on a screen. Every other action is a secondary (outlined), ghost (underlined) or danger (red outline) button.

**The Colour-Is-Never-Alone Rule.** A status colour never appears without its mark and a text label.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bodoni Moda Variable (with Didot, Bodoni 72, serif)
**Body Font:** Archivo Variable (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Label/Mono Font:** Martian Mono Variable (with ui-monospace, SF Mono)

**Character:** A high-contrast Didone prints the money, like the denomination on a note. A width-flexing grotesque does all the reading and the controls, and it widens (the wdth axis, 100 to 125) as it gets louder. A narrow mono appears only where a real serial exists.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Bodoni 600, clamp 3.25 to 5.5rem, 0.92): the pot figure on the note face. It is always the largest element on the page. The compact note uses display-compact (clamp 2.25 to 3rem).
- **Headline** (Archivo 720, wdth 112, clamp 1.625 to 2.125rem, 1.12): page titles (h1).
- **Title** (Archivo 680, wdth 106, 1.25rem): section heads (h2). Title-sm (650, wdth 104, 1rem) is used for h3.
- **Body** (Archivo 400, 1rem, 1.5): reading text. Leads cap at 62ch. Body-sm (0.9375rem) is used for secondary lines, notices and ledger rows.
- **Label** (Archivo 560, 0.875rem): status-mark labels and metadata.
- **Note issuer** (Archivo 760, wdth 125, 0.875rem, 0.1em, uppercase, in series ink): the group's name printed on its own note face. **Stamp** (800, wdth 125, 0.18em, uppercase) is used for the overprinted HELD, ISSUED and SPECIMEN stamps.
- **Serial** (Martian Mono, wdth 87, 0.8125rem, 0.02em, tabular): invite codes (KUTU-XXXX), round numbers (R04/10) and ledger references. **Microprint** (Martian Mono 5px, uppercase) is used for the printed rule on the note face.

### Named Rules
**The Real-Serial Rule.** Martian Mono sets only real references: an invite code, a round number, a ledger ref. It never sets labels, headings or decorative text.

**The Pot-Is-Largest Rule.** Bodoni sets only money figures on notes and the rosette count. Nothing on a screen is set larger than the pot.

**The Letterspaced-Caps-Belong-To-The-Note Rule.** Uppercase tracked text appears only as printed matter on the note (issuer line, stamp, microprint) and in serial inputs. It is never used as a label or heading above page content.

## Layout

The layout is a single column on phones and becomes a rail plus content at 900px and up. The rail is 232px wide and sticky, with a hairline right rule. It holds the brand mark, the primary navigation and "Your groups", with each group shown by its series swatch. Below 900px the rail is replaced by a fixed three-item bottom tab bar (64px, plus the safe-area inset) on translucent raised paper.

Content is capped at 1200px and centred. The gutter is 16px and widens to 32px at 720px. Section gaps are 28px and widen to 36px at 900px. On the group page at desktop width, the note and the ledger sit side by side, divided by a hairline rule. Modals centre up to 520px wide and become a bottom sheet at 520px and below. At 680px and below, the ledger table re-flows into stacked rows (entry and amount, then date, then ref). The spacing rhythm is 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32px, with 6, 10 and 14px used inside components.

## Elevation & Depth

The app is mostly flat and ruled. Depth belongs to paper objects: the note face sits on the page with a soft lift, and the modal sheet floats above a dimmed paper overlay. Everything else uses hairline rules and paper tone (raised or sunk) instead of shadows.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(21,25,30,0.06), 0 8px 24px -12px rgba(21,25,30,0.18)`): note faces at rest.
- **Sheet** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(21,25,30,0.08), 0 24px 64px -24px rgba(21,25,30,0.35)`): the modal, and a note link on hover (together with a 2px rise).
- **Window** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 3px ink at 14%, inset 0 0 0 0.75px series line`): the see-through oval window on the note.
- In night print, both shadows switch to black-based values (see sidecar).

### Named Rules
**The Paper-Objects-Only Rule.** Only notes and sheets cast shadows. Rows, notices, fields and tabs are separated by rules and tone.

## Shapes

Corners are gently rounded: 6px on controls, 10px on notes, sheets and empty states. The note carries its own geometry, which nothing else borrows: a 50% oval window, a rosette of elliptical petals (one per member), guilloche bands top and bottom, and a tilted (-9deg) rectangular stamp with a 2.5px border and 4px corners. Series swatches are small upright rectangles (2px corners) shaped like a note seen edge-on. Circles are used only for avatars, status marks, the switch knob and the rail's owes ring.

## Components

### Buttons
Printed and plain. Nothing glows.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (6px), minimum height 44px, padding 10px 18px, 1px border.
- **Primary:** solid ink with raised-paper text. There is one per screen.
- **Hover / Focus:** hover shifts the fill to action-hover. Pressing drops the button 1px. Focus shows a 2px focus-blue outline offset by 2px. Transitions are 160ms ease-out.
- **Secondary:** raised paper with a rule-strong border that darkens to ink on hover.
- **Ghost:** a transparent text button with a rule-strong underline that inks on hover.
- **Danger:** transparent, with failed-red text and a 45% red border. Hover fills it with failed-tint.
- **Disabled primary:** sunk paper with ink-3 text and a rule border.

### Status Mark
The system's replacement for pills. It is a 16px SVG mark plus a text label (0.875rem, 560), with the mark and label in the status colour:
- **Filled seal with a check** = paid (paid).
- **Half-filled seal** = paid late (late).
- **Open ring** = unpaid (ink-2).
- **Struck ring** = failed (failed).
- **Small dot** = not due yet (rule-strong).

In the payments grid, a member's payout round is shown by an ink-outlined cell. The current round column gets a neutral sunk-paper wash, never series colour.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** raised paper, 1px rule-strong border, 6px radius, minimum height 48px, 12px 14px inner padding. The label (0.9375rem, 620) sits above the field, with an optional ink-2 hint below it. A prefix such as "+60" or "RM" sits inside the control.
- **Focus:** the border turns ink and a 3px focus-blue halo appears at 22%.
- **Error:** a failed-red border, plus an icon and text in failed red below.
- **Serial inputs:** invite-code fields use uppercase and 0.08em tracking.
- **OTP and Stepper** controls use the same shell. The stepper divides its value cell with hairline rules.

### Navigation
- **Rail (900px and up):** items are 42px high with 6px corners, set in ink-2 at 580 weight. Hover gives ink text on sunk paper. The active item is raised paper with an inset 1px rule. Groups are listed with their series swatch, and a late-amber ring marks groups where you owe a payment.
- **Tab bar (below 900px):** three columns with an icon above a 0.75rem label. Inactive items are ink-3. The active item is ink with a heavier icon stroke.
- **Tabs:** set in ink-2 at 600 weight, wdth 104, over a hairline base rule. The active tab is ink with a 2px ink underline. Tabs never use pill or segmented backgrounds.

### Cards / Containers
There are no generic cards. The containers are:
- **Notice:** raised paper, rule border, 6px corners, 14px 16px padding, with an icon, a title and body, and an optional trailing action. The warning variant tints the border late amber. The failed variant fills with failed-tint.
- **Empty state:** a dashed rule-strong border with 10px corners on translucent raised paper.
- **Modal sheet:** see Layout and Elevation.
- **Person rows and ledger rows:** hairline-ruled rows with no card around them.

### Ledger
A ruled table. The column heads are ink-3 over a 1.5px ink rule. Month group headers are bold (wdth 110) over a rule-strong line. Amounts use tabular figures. Contributions are paid green with a "+" and payouts are ink. References are set in serial. A small series swatch names the group on each row.

### Note Face (signature)
The group's current round printed as a note. It has series-tint paper, a 1px border in series ink at 38%, 10px corners and the lift shadow. A guilloche underprint in series line sits at 55% opacity, with guilloche bands top and bottom and a microprint rule. The layout is:
- **Header:** the issuer line on the left and the serial (invite code and round) on the right.
- **Middle:** the pot label and the Bodoni pot figure in series ink, with a small wide "RM".
- **Window:** an oval see-through window holding "Pays out [date] to" and the recipient's name.
- **Rosette:** one elliptical petal per member. Paid petals are inked in series ink and unpaid petals are dashed ghosts in ink-3 at 32%. The count ("7 of 10 paid") sits in the hollow and shortens to "7/10" in small rosettes.

Overprinted stamps mark HELD (failed), ISSUED (paid) and SPECIMEN (series ink at 85%). A compact variant is used on the home list and in the wizard preview. Petals ink in over 1100ms, staggered 70ms apart, and the microprint clips in over 1400ms. Both run once. Reduced motion removes both.

### Avatar
A circular initial on sunk paper with a rule border and ink-2 text. The current member ("You") is inverted to solid ink.

### Switch
A 46x28px track on sunk paper with a raised knob. When on, the track fills with paid green.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep series colour on the note face and on note-shaped swatches only (12x16px in the rail, 8x11px in the ledger).
- **Do** pair every status with its mark and a text label: filled seal = paid, half-filled = paid late, open ring = unpaid, struck ring = failed, small dot = not due yet.
- **Do** set exactly one primary action per screen in solid ink (action). Every other action is secondary, ghost or danger.
- **Do** set the pot in Bodoni Moda as the largest figure on the page, and set invite codes, round numbers and ledger references in Martian Mono.
- **Do** derive a group's series from its id through `seriesClass()`. Use specimen only together with a SPECIMEN stamp.
- **Do** design every surface for night print as well, using the night-* tokens and the lifted series.
- **Do** separate content with hairline rules (rule, rule-strong) and paper tone, not with boxes and shadows.

### Don't:
- **Don't** tint tables, lists, chips, tabs or current-round highlights with series colour. Use ink or neutral.
- **Don't** use KPI stat tiles or dashboard metric cards.
- **Don't** use pills or badge chips for status. Use the status mark.
- **Don't** communicate status with colour alone.
- **Don't** add a seventh series or a saturated sixth hue. Series 5 stays neutral charcoal, because the remaining hues collide with paid, late or another series.
- **Don't** set a second solid-ink button on the same screen.
- **Don't** use tracked uppercase labels or eyebrows above page content. Tracked caps belong to printed matter on the note.
